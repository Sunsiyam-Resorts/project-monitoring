## Direction contract

THESIS: The portal's look comes from the reference image the user supplied: a sage-green sidebar, a black workspace and charcoal cards, with green pill buttons. It replaces the earlier frosted-glass and spreadsheet-grid look. Layout, navigation and controls stay as they are.

OWN-WORLD: Dark: near-black canvas #0b0b0b, charcoal cards #1a1a1a, hairlines #2b2b2b, white ink, sage #a2d397 for primary pills, active marks and focus. Light: the same sage sidebar with a pale canvas #eef1ec, white cards and dark ink. Dark text sits on every sage fill. Status chips are small solid blocks with dark text. Buttons are pills. Cards have an 10px radius and no glass.

STORY: Staff see the green rail, the page title and their figures first. The primary action is always the green pill at the top right of the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The sage rail on the left holds the logo, company scope, the navigation (active item as a full-width darker band, counts in black discs) and a black account card at the bottom. On the right are the page title and subtitle, with a dark pill and a green pill on the right, then charcoal cards with white figures.
